About Us

Empowering Small & Medium Enterprises

TBX Business Manager Pro is a premier management system crafted specifically to provide entrepreneurs, small business owners, and growing enterprises with full operational control.

Designed with offline capabilities, TBX Business Manager Pro eliminates complex cloud overhead while offering professional-grade POS, inventory, CRM, financial tracking, and localized AI intelligence.

Core Pillars

  • Complete Data Sovereignty: Your records remain locally stored on your browser environment.
  • Real-Time Analytics: Instant financial visualizers and stock alerts to keep operations running smoothly.
  • Embedded AI Intelligence: Automated business health diagnostics running entirely in local memory.

Privacy Policy

1. Storage Privacy Architecture

TBX Business Manager Pro operates under a local-first architecture. All records, ledger balances, transactions, inventory levels, and customer contact information are stored directly in your browser's local environment.

2. Data Transmission Transparency

We do not track, collect, or monetise any of your commercial or personal business records. The system functions without reaching out to third-party tracking servers for baseline operational processing.

3. User Authority & Data Portability

You maintain full operational authority over your records. You may export full JSON backups or execute database resets at any time through the Backup & Restore module.

User Agreement / Terms of Service

1. Terms Acceptance

By launching and utilizing TBX Business Manager Pro, you agree to comply with this User Agreement. The software is provided for enterprise operational tracking and organizational management.

2. Data Stewardship Responsibility

Because your business records exist on your device environment, you are responsible for creating regular backups using the built-in export utility. TBX Business Manager Pro is not liable for data loss caused by clearing browser storage or unexpected device failure.

3. Usage Authorization

You agree to utilize this application in compliance with local commercial regulations in Kerugoya, Kenya, and your operating jurisdiction.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: How do I backup my business records?

A: Navigate to Backup & Restore in the sidebar and click Backup Data (Download File) to save a copy of your records as a JSON backup file.


Q: Can staff members perform admin resets?

A: No. System privileges prevent non-admin staff operators from executing data purges or modifying security policies.
